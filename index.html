02 / ABOUT

About me.

✳FROM NEPAL.
NOW IN MISSISSIPPI.

I’m Aayush, a Computer Engineering student with a Mathematics minor at the University of Southern Mississippi. My interests sit where computer architecture, memory systems, and efficient AI meet.

I like figuring out what’s slowing things down and making better use of the hardware that’s already there. Mostly by building things, measuring them, and trying again.

Beyond the lab, I’m a two-time national mathematics olympiad finalist and represented Nepal at the 2025 IOMHSS in Turkmenistan. I also founded VIDYA, helping students across Nepal discover academic opportunities.

Outside of work, I like playing football. I support Real Madrid.
